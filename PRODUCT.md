# Product

register: brand

## Users
Egyptians living abroad, and Egyptian residents who cannot visit a government office. They need an official paper (birth, family record, marriage) and today depend on a relative, a broker, or a trip home. They arrive on a phone, often in the evening, often in Arabic, wanting to know the full price and the timeline before committing.

## Product Purpose
Inzerv is a digital concierge: the user submits a request and uploads IDs, the team in Egypt obtains the document from the authorities, and it is delivered in Egypt or abroad. Status is trackable end to end. The website sells the service and hosts the same request flow as the mobile app (same backend API). Government Documents is the only live category; Real Estate, Transportation, Healthcare and Offers are planned.

## Brand
Trust, Simplicity, Modernity. Tagline (never reworded): "Designed with purpose. Built on trust. Made for you." Voice: plain, calm, specific. Says what happens next and what it costs. Never hype.

## Tone and constraints
- Navy #062544, Turquoise #10B7BE, White. Supporting: #031A30, #F5F7F9, #667785. Light mode primary.
- Inter (EN) and IBM Plex Sans Arabic (AR) only. Weights 400/500/600.
- Turquoise buttons use Navy text, never white.
- No gradients, glow, neon, heavy shadows. No per-service accent colors.
- Full RTL for Arabic; brand marks are never mirrored.
- Only approved logo assets. Never redraw the logo.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing pages with invented ratings, download counts, testimonials or logo bars. Fintech navy-and-gold. Purple/neon AI looks.

## Strategic principles
- Show real facts only: real prices, real destinations, real statuses. No invented numbers.
- Price transparency before sign-up.
- Every nav item and button leads somewhere real.
